Joining the Boğaziçi University Computer Engineering Department means becoming a part of one of the most prestigious and respected academic families in Turkey. This department will prepare you for a successful career in computer engineering by providing you with a strong theoretical foundation and sought-after practical skills in the industry.

## Why Computer Engineering? {#why-cmpe}

Computer engineering is a deeply rewarding profession, built on creativity and mathematical thinking, that helps shape the technology and the world of the future, and one that will remain in demand across the world throughout the next 20 to 30 years of your working life. As the industrial age gave way to the information age, it emerged as the profession that makes that age possible. The need for computer engineers grows every year, and graduates of computer engineering readily find excellent positions. The world's leading advanced economies owe much of their edge to the strides they have made in this field, which is why they invest heavily in computing and work to attract the brightest minds to it. We want the brightest young people of our country to turn to computer engineering as well.

## Why BOUN CMPE? {#why-bouncmpe}

The Boğaziçi University Department of Computer Engineering, `bouncmpe` for short, is a leading department recognized around the world and the finest computer engineering department in Turkey. With 20 full-time and 1 part-time faculty members holding doctorates, and close to 50 research assistants, it has the strongest academic staff in the country. Measured by its faculty members' research and publications, it is the computer engineering department with the highest publication performance in Turkey. Our faculty members lead a wide range of research projects supported by the European Union and other international sources as well as by national research institutions. Each year our faculty and staff win awards worldwide and receive best paper awards for their publications, while our students place in a variety of competitions with their projects. Our graduates are admitted with scholarships to the world's best universities for graduate study, both in Turkey and abroad, and go on to hold and grow into important positions at the sector's leading companies.

**We invite you to choose the profession of the future and to join this dynamic department.**
