Our doctoral graduate Suzan Ece Ada's PhD dissertation titled "Robust and Adaptive Deep Reinforcement Learning" has been awarded the DOCTORAL THESIS AWARD by the Boğaziçi University Scientific Research Projects Commission due to its outstanding scientific quality. Dr. Ada, a former member of the CoLoRs Lab, conducted her thesis under the supervision of Assoc. Prof. Dr. Emre Uğur, and her impactful research has gained strong international recognition with high-profile publications and presentations at leading global venues such as NeurIPS, ICRA, and IROS.
