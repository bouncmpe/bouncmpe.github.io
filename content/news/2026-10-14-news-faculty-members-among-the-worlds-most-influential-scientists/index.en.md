In the [World's Most Influential Scientists List](https://elsevier.digitalcommonsdata.com/datasets/btchxktzyw/9) published by Stanford University in collaboration with Elsevier, Prof. Lale Akarun, Prof. Fatih Alagöz and Prof. Cem Ersoy are listed in the Career-Long Impact category, while Prof. Cem Ersoy and Asst. Prof. Muhammed Göleç are listed in the Single-Year Impact category.

We congratulate our faculty members on this achievement.
