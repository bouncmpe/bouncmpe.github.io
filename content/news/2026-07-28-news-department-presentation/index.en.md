On Wednesday, 29 July at 1:30 PM, the Computer Engineering Department will hold its introductory presentation and department tour. As part of the event, we will bring our prospective students together with our alumni. We warmly welcome all prospective students and their families to the Computer Engineering Department Building at the Boğaziçi University North Campus.
