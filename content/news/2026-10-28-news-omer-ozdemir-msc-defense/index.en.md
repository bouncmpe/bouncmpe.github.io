Our Master's student Ömer Özdemir successfully defended his MSc thesis titled "Behavioral Analysis of Ethereum Blockchain Transactions" on October 28, 2026. The thesis committee consisted of Prof. Dr. Can Özturan (Boğaziçi University), Prof. Dr. Arzucan Özgür (Boğaziçi University), and Prof. Dr. Öznur Özkasap (Koç University).

A study based on the thesis, titled "Behavioral Analysis of Ethereum Blockchain Transactions with LLM-based User Profiling", has been accepted as a full paper at the 2026 8th IEEE International Conference on Blockchain Computing and Applications (BCCA 2026), which will be held on November 16–20, 2026, in Barcelona, Spain.

Our Department congratulates Ömer Özdemir on this achievement and wishes him continued success in his future endeavors.
