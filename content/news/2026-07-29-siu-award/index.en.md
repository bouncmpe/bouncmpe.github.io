Our Master's student Refika Kalyoncu has been awarded the First Place Award in the Robotics, Control, and Automation Track at the 34th Conference on Signal Processing and Communications Applications with her work titled "Discrete Relational Symbol Learning for Combined Object Affordances". We congratulate our student and her advisor Assoc. Prof. Dr. Emre Uğur.
